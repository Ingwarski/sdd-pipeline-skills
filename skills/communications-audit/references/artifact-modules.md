# Artifact-Specific Audit Modules

Apply the universal seven dimensions first, then use only the relevant modules below. These checks refine the evidence; they do not add separate scored dimensions.

## Websites and landing pages

- Crawl every unique public same-domain HTML page unless the user limits scope.
- Inventory URLs by template and journey role: entry, education, service/product, proof, comparison, pricing, conversion, support, and utility.
- Audit repeated templates through representative pages while preserving full-site coverage in the inventory.
- Check homepage and landing-page orientation, audience recognition, offer clarity, information scent, message hierarchy, proof, objection handling, CTA clarity, conversion continuity, cross-page consistency, contact paths, form language, and desktop/mobile comprehension.
- Trace at least the primary conversion journey from entry to the final public step.
- Treat broken messaging hierarchy or unusable calls to action as communication findings. Refer technical SEO, AI-discoverability, accessibility, performance, security, and legal matters to specialist audits.

## Sales decks, proposals, one-pagers, and leave-behinds

- Inspect the complete document, including notes, appendices, pricing, disclaimers, and calls to action when present.
- Check whether the opening earns attention, the buyer's situation is represented accurately, the narrative progresses logically, the value proposition is specific, proof supports claims, objections are answered, commercial terms are understandable, and the next step is unmistakable.
- Evaluate scanability for both executive readers and operational evaluators.
- Check whether feature language is translated into audience-relevant outcomes without unsupported promises.

## Email and outbound sequences

- Inspect the entire sequence and its intended cadence, not one message in isolation.
- Check sender credibility, audience specificity, subject and preview clarity, relevance of the opening, message length, proof, ask size, CTA friction, follow-up logic, tone, and sequence progression.
- Evaluate timing against journey stage and current context. Treat technical deliverability as a specialist follow-up unless explicitly in scope.
- Flag personalization that is false, invasive, generic, or unsupported.

## Advertising and social content

- Check hook, audience recognition, offer, claim credibility, creative-copy congruence, emotional and rational value, platform context, CTA, and landing-page continuity.
- Evaluate each placement in the campaign sequence when multiple assets are supplied.
- Separate a communication problem from media-buying, attribution, or platform-operations problems.

## Brochures and marketing collateral

- Check physical or digital reading context, scan order, value hierarchy, proof, specification-to-benefit translation, navigation, contact details, CTA, and handoff to the next channel.
- Evaluate whether dense information is structured for lookup instead of forcing linear reading.
- Preserve regulated or technical language when accuracy requires it; recommend a clearer explanatory layer rather than deleting necessary detail.

## Audio, video, commercials, webinars, and recorded pitches

- Inspect the media when available. If only a transcript exists, state that visual, vocal, and pacing judgments are limited.
- Check the opening hook, speaker identity and credibility, audience relevance, spoken message, structure, pace, tone, emphasis, story, visual-verbal congruence, proof, duration, channel fit, and CTA.
- Evaluate nonverbal or paraverbal execution only from actual media evidence.
- Keep editing, production, sound mixing, and media generation outside scope unless separately requested.

## Mixed campaigns

- Map the audience journey across assets and channels.
- Identify the role of each artifact, handoffs between artifacts, repeated or contradictory claims, missing proof, CTA discontinuity, and timing gaps.
- Use one blended weight profile tied to the campaign's primary effect.
- Avoid averaging unrelated artifacts into a score that hides a broken critical step. Show material asset-level exceptions.
